# Emerging AI Careers

These are roles to investigate, not a claim that all titles are new or standardized.

| Role | Research focus |
| --- | --- |
| AI Business Analyst | Translate business needs into AI-related requirements and measurable outcomes |
| AI Product Analyst | Analyze product usage and outcomes for AI-enabled products |
| Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) | Work with customers to implement and adapt technical solutions |
| AI Solutions Consultant | Help organizations evaluate and adopt AI solutions |
| Data Analyst (AI-focused) | Analyze data used to evaluate AI tools, products, or workflows |

For each role, record a dated job posting, company, location, responsibilities, required skills, and portfolio evidence requested.
