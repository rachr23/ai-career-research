# Skills & Qualifications

## Skills to look for in actual job postings

- Data analysis and visualization
- SQL and spreadsheet analysis
- Business requirements gathering
- Communication with technical and nontechnical stakeholders
- AI literacy and evaluation of model outputs
- Product and business metrics
- Python or coding requirements, where applicable

**Research question:** Which skills are consistently required, and which vary by role?

Do not label a skill mandatory until verified against a specific employer's posting.
